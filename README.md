# *Project Overview* #

Interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor all warehouse operations, inventory performance, and productivity metrics through a centralized analytical view.

The solution consolidates operational data into a single dashboard, enabling faster decision-making and greater visibility into key warehouse performance indicators. Specifically, it provides greater control over operational resources hired during peak seasons (due to events like Black Friday, Cyber ​​Monday, Christmas, and Singles' Day) or during off-peak seasons.

# *Business Objective* #

Warehouse leaders typically need quick access to visual, real-time operational metrics for daily decision-making, with a strong focus on objective data to ensure the best decisions, aiming to eliminate downtime, poor operational performance, and prepare for ISO 13485 certification.

This dashboard was developed to:

- Monitor warehouse productivity.
- Track operational performance.
- Analyze processing trends.
- Identify capacity constraints.
- Improve visibility of inventory-related activities.

# *Potential Business Insight* #

- Operational visibility enabled a **50% increase in picking throughput.**
- The volume of orders processed **increased from 120 to 180 orders, generating a 50% increase in operational throughput**.
- The average **picking time per order was reduced from 3 minutes to 1 minute, representing an approximate 66.7% improvement in process efficiency**.
- With the same team of 4 pickers operating 12-hour shifts, **processing capacity increased by 60 additional orders per day**.
- Considering an average value of USD $5 per order, **the processed value increased from USD $600 to USD $900 per day, equivalent to an increase of USD $300 (+50%)**.

Centralizing operational KPIs allowed for:

- Real-time identification of bottlenecks.
- Monitoring of hourly employee productivity.
- Early detection of operational deviations.
- Increased processing capacity without increasing staff.
- Improve warehouse response speed.
